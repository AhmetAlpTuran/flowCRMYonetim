Privacy Policy

Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · This is a translation; the Turkish version prevails.

In short: Flow CRM lets businesses talk to their customers on WhatsApp, use an AI assistant and send campaigns. We process account data to provide the service, and we process the businesses' customer data only on their behalf and instructions. We do not sell data or use it for advertising or profiling. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Controller and our roles

[COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

  • As controller for the account, membership and usage data of business owners and employees using the app ("Users").
  • As processor for the contact, message and media data of the people businesses talk to on WhatsApp ("End Users"). The business is the controller of that data; if you messaged a business, please contact that business first. We forward requests we receive to the business.

2. Data we process

Users

  • Email address and display name; password (stored only as a one-way hash); sessions and password-reset codes.
  • Business memberships, roles, module permissions and invitations.
  • Action records (who assigned, handed over or recorded consent, and when) and technical error records (request ID and error code; no message content).
  • WhatsApp connection identifiers and verified business name; the access token is stored encrypted.
  • Subscription and billing: billing details entered by a business administrator (name, email, mobile number, Turkish ID or tax number, company name, tax office, billing address), plan and payment status, and the payment provider's subscription references. Card details never reach Flow CRM; they are entered in iyzico's secure payment form.

End Users

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name, plus name, email and tags added by the business.
  • Message content and timestamps; photos, voice notes, videos and documents exchanged.
  • Marketing consent status, its source and basis, who recorded it, and opt-out dates.
  • Support records (handoffs, assignment, resolution times) and, if used, bookings and leads.

The app does not use advertising identifiers, location, contacts access or third-party tracking.

3. Purposes and legal bases (KVKK Art. 5)

  • Account, sign-in, password reset, business and team management — performance of contract.
  • Receiving, showing and sending WhatsApp messages and storing media — performance of contract; End User data on behalf of the business.
  • AI-assisted replies, if the business enables them — performance of contract, on the business's behalf.
  • Campaigns and consent records — legal obligation under Turkish Law No. 6563, on the business's behalf.
  • Security, abuse prevention and action records — legitimate interest and legal obligation.
  • Billing, once paid plans launch — performance of contract and tax obligations.

4. AI assistant

If a business enables the assistant, the customer's latest message, a limited number of earlier messages in the same conversation, relevant parts of the business's knowledge base and its bot settings are sent to OpenAI's API to draft a reply. Photos, audio and files are not sent to the AI; those messages go to an employee. Requests are sent with storage disabled; OpenAI does not use API data for training by default and may retain it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. The assistant hands the conversation to a person when unsure or when asked.

  • Supabase Inc. — database, authentication, file storage and server functions — [HOSTING REGION].
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — message delivery — USA and other countries.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — AI replies, if enabled — USA.
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] — verification and password-reset emails.
  • Google (Firebase) / Apple — notifications (once enabled) and app distribution.
  • iyzico Ödeme Hizmetleri A.Ş. — subscription payments and card processing, as a payment institution — Turkey.

Transfers abroad are made under Article 9 of KVKK, using the standard contracts announced by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board or other appropriate safeguards. We otherwise share data only with authorities legally entitled to request it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • User account: until deleted. Delete it in the app under Account menu > Delete account. Businesses where you are the only member are deleted with all their data; other memberships are removed.
  • Business data: until the business is deleted or for the period it sets.
  • Consent and opt-out records: for the period required by law, as evidence.
  • Backups and technical records: up to [BACKUP RETENTION, e.g. 30 days].
  • Invoices: for the period required by tax law.

7. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never sent to the app. Each business's data is isolated by database access rules, and employees see only the modules they are permitted to use. Files are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived, permission-checked links. Important actions are logged and WhatsApp callbacks are signature-verified.

8. Businesses' responsibilities

Businesses using Flow CRM are controllers of their customers' data. They must inform their customers, obtain consent before sending commercial messages under Law No. 6563 (and register it in the İYS system where required), record opt-outs in the app, and answer their customers' data requests.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and recipients, request correction or deletion, object to outcomes based solely on automated processing, and claim compensation for unlawful processing. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL]; we respond free of charge within 30 days. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

10. Children

Flow CRM is a business service and is not intended for people under 18.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will announce material changes in the app or by email. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
